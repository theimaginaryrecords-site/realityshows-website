Siwet Tomar Clarifies Gautam Gulati Remark: "It Was Meant as a Joke"

September 30, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Amid the storm over his reported removal, Siwet Tomar has clarified that his remarks about Gautam Gulati were "meant as a joke", according to TellyChakkar.

In a separate conversation reported by the outlet, Siwet also said he felt "betrayed" by fellow contestant Niharika Tiwari — adding a personal fallout to the season's growing list of fractures.

With his future on the show uncertain and official word awaited, Siwet's off-screen clarifications are doing little to calm the controversy.
