Khatron Ke Khiladi 15 Winner: Avinash Mishra Conquers Fear to Lift the Trophy, Beats Rithvik Dhanjani and Orry

By Aabid Khan · October 13, 2026

Television actor Avinash Mishra has been crowned the winner of Khatron Ke Khiladi 15, conquering the grand finale that aired on Sunday, October 4. He defeated Rithvik Dhanjani, who finished as first runner-up, and Orhan Awatramani aka Orry, the second runner-up, in a nerve-shredding final stunt.

The decisive task pushed the three finalists to their absolute limits — a helicopter jump followed by a jet-ski ride and a swim to the endpoint, capped with a blast. Avinash completed the high-risk course in the fastest time, sealing a victory that had been building all season.

Along with the coveted trophy, Avinash took home a cash prize of ₹20 lakh, a brand-new Maruti Suzuki Swift, a trip to Europe and a bike — one of the richest winner packages in the show's history.

The moment overwhelmed him. As host Rohit Shetty announced his name, Avinash broke down in tears, saying that in ten years of television he had been nominated for characters and attended award functions, but this was the first award he had ever actually won.

His run through the Cape Town season was the stuff of champion montages. Entering as a fresh challenger in the show's 'Champions vs Challengers' format, Avinash steadily grew into the season's strongest performer — becoming its very first Top 5 finalist after outperforming veteran Karan Wahi in a technically brutal underwater task, captaining his rookie squad through the high-octane Team Week twist, and earning a signed custom Fear Jacket from Rohit Shetty himself.

Karan Wahi, who made the grand finale in his third appearance on the show (after Season 8 and 'Made in India'), was eliminated just before the final top-three round. He later posted a heartfelt note calling the season 'sooo much more than a show' and praising Shetty as the man behind the journey.

The celebrations spilled off-screen. Avinash threw a victory party for friends and industry colleagues — though his Bigg Boss 18 co-contestant Vivian Dsena was noticeably absent. Vivian later explained on Instagram that he had been 'genuinely quite unwell' and not up to socialising, promising a long-overdue reunion soon. Meanwhile Eisha Singh, who was present at the finale, celebrated with Avinash at home, the two dancing to 'Senorita' as she posted 'Trophy aa gayi ghar!'

For Avinash — third runner-up on Bigg Boss 18 — the KKK15 crown marks a full-circle redemption: the reality-show bridesmaid is finally the champion.
