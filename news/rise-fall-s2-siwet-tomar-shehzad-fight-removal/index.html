Violence Rocks Rise and Fall S2: Siwet Tomar Reportedly Removed After Shehzad Clash

September 29, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Rise and Fall Season 2 has its first major scandal. During the "Ultimate Ruler race" task, Siwet Tomar allegedly grabbed Shehzad Poonawalla by the neck, pushed him down and tore his kurta, according to reports in Bombay Times, Oneindia and MensXP.

The trigger, per reports: Shehzad called Siwet the "weakest decision-maker", and things escalated — with a religion-card accusation also reportedly flying in the heat of the moment.

Siwet was reportedly removed as the first contestant to leave, and Swara Bhasker and Karan Patel reportedly walked out in protest. Critical caveat: the makers' official confirmation of the removal and walkouts had not been independently verified at the time of writing.

Physical violence is a red line on Indian reality TV — if confirmed, this becomes the defining moment of the season.
