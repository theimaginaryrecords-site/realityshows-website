Shock Mid-Week Eviction: Rhiti Tiwari Out of Bigg Boss 20

October 2, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Bigg Boss 20 witnessed its most shocking twist yet on October 1, as singer Rhiti Tiwari was evicted mid-week — not by public vote, but by a decision of her fellow captaincy contenders.

According to reports, Mahhi Vij, Yung DSA and Uditi Singh were called to the confession room and asked to name the nominated housemate contributing the least to the game. Mahhi named Mary Kom, Yung named Aman Gandhi, and Uditi named Rhiti — calling her "personality confusing." The other two reportedly agreed with Uditi's pick, making the decision unanimous.

The eviction came just days after Salman Khan tore into Rhiti on Weekend Ka Vaar over her language in the diss-battle task, calling it one of the "worst disses" he had ever seen. Eleven housemates had been nominated that week.

As news broke, fans flooded social media with theories — including speculation that Rhiti may have been moved to a secret room rather than truly evicted. There is no official confirmation of any secret room; such theories remain fan speculation for now.
