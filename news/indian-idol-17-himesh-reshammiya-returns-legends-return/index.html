Himesh Reshammiya Returns as Indian Idol 17 Judge; 'Legends Return' Promo Out

September 27, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Indian Idol 17: Legends Return is officially on the way — the first promo was unveiled September 25, 2026, confirming Himesh Reshammiya's return as judge (he judged Seasons 11-13).

The rest of the panel and the host are still TBA — one unconfirmed report suggests Aditya Narayan is tied up with Rise and Fall commitments, but that is not verified.

The singing reality giant airs on Sony Entertainment Television and SonyLIV; the premiere date is awaited. Once auditions begin, expect this page to become poll central.
