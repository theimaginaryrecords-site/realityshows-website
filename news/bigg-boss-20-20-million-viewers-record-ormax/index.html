History Created: Bigg Boss 20 Crosses 20 Million Weekly Viewers

September 24, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Bigg Boss 20 is officially a phenomenon. The show crossed 20 million weekly streaming viewers in the week ending September 20, becoming the first non-cricket property in 2026 to hit the milestone, according to Ormax StreamView data.

The growth has been relentless: 6.4 million on launch day (September 6, the biggest reality opening day ever on JioHotstar), 16.8 million in the September 7-13 week, and now past 20 million — a jump of over 3 million in a single week.

The season topped every other reality property that week, with the Hindi edition drawing four to five times the audience of its Tamil and Telugu counterparts. Salman's viral shirtless anti-troll moment is widely credited with fuelling the surge.

A note on the numbers: BARC television ratings are currently suspended, so these are OTT/streaming figures from JioHotstar only — not a combined TV-plus-OTT audience.
