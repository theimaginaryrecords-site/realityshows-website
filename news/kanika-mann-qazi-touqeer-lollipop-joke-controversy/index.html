"That Was Cheap": Kanika Mann Slams Qazi Touqeer Over Lollipop Remark

October 1, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Bigg Boss 20's latest flashpoint came during a Teacher-Student task, when Qazi Touqeer — playing a student — told Kanika Mann, "I want to study Biology," and followed it with, "You are so beautiful ma'am, can we have lollipop together."

Kanika confronted him directly: "That was cheap, Qazi Touqeer. I don't expect cheap things from you. Tameez sikhaake jaungi. It's cheap." She made it clear she understood the double meaning and was not a child.

Qazi claimed he was in character, comparing his act to Hrithik Roshan's Koi... Mil Gaya character — "kids like lollipop." Aman Gandhi also called out Qazi's manner of speaking. The friction is not new: Kanika had named Qazi her least favourite contestant back in premiere week.

The exchange has split the internet, with many praising Kanika for drawing a firm line. Expect Salman Khan to weigh in on Weekend Ka Vaar.
