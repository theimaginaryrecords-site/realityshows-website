Gaurav Khanna Quits KKK15 Mid-Season Despite Winning His Elimination Stunt

September 30, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

In one of the season's most emotional moments, Gaurav Khanna broke down on Khatron Ke Khiladi 15, revealing how the "Panauti" tag had haunted his career — then quit the show mid-season over an elbow injury.

The twist: Khanna quit despite winning his elimination stunt, choosing to prioritise his health over continuing. His injury video later went viral, prompting the makers to respond that the stunts were "not at all brutal" and all safety protocols were followed.

Khanna's vulnerability about the Panauti label — and his decision to walk away on his own terms — earned him widespread sympathy from fans who had watched him struggle through the season.
