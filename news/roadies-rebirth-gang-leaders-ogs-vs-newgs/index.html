OGs vs NewGs: Meet the 6 Gang Leaders of Roadies Rebirth

September 27, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

The battle lines are drawn. Roadies Rebirth's six gang leaders: OGs — Nikhil Chinapa, Neha Dhupia, Prince Narula; NewGs — Abhishek Malhan (Fukra Insaan), Bani J, Baseer Ali.

It is a delicious setup — the architects of classic Roadies against the new-age reality stars who grew up watching it. Neha and Prince bring championship pedigree; Fukra Insaan brings the biggest digital army in the game.

One shadow over the announcement: Prince Narula's spot has been the subject of replacement rumours (see our fact-check). For now, he is in.
