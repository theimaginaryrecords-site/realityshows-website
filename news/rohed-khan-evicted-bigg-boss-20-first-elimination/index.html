Rohed Khan Becomes First Eviction of Bigg Boss 20

September 21, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Rohed Khan became the first contestant evicted from Bigg Boss 20 on September 20, exiting during Weekend Ka Vaar after receiving the fewest public votes.

The nomination itself was dramatic: Rohed, Aasif Khan, ScoutOP and captain Yung DSA were accused of discussing nominations — against the rules — so Bigg Boss cancelled the regular process and directly nominated the trio (Yung was exempt as captain).

Salman Khan praised Rohed's grace on exit: "Job well done. Well played with dignity." Rohed said appearing on stage with Salman had been his dream; his close friends Rhiti and Aasif turned emotional at his departure.

Rohed had already burned one of his two "Extra Jeevan Daan" lifelines earlier in a captaincy task — reports differ on which contender sacrificed it.
