Isha Rikhi's Explosive Claims About Ex-Husband Badshah: "Cheated In Every Way"

September 29, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Days before entering the Bigg Boss 20 house, Isha Rikhi and rapper Badshah announced a mutual separation on August 31 — a split that raised eyebrows, given their surprise wedding earlier in 2026 had already sparked curiosity. Badshah never posted wedding photos; only Isha's family shared them.

Inside the house, Isha opened up to Kanika Mann, saying she realised "you were being played the whole time." When asked whether the alleged cheating was physical, emotional or social, she reportedly replied: "All of them" — also claiming Badshah had "too many best friends."

These are Isha's allegations; Badshah has not publicly responded to the specific claims. Shortly after her eviction, he posted a "thanks fans" reel.

The revelations have dominated gossip columns since her September 27 eviction, adding an off-screen drama track to her three-week stint.
