Captaincy Task Turns Ugly: Alliances Crack in Week 4

September 29, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

The Week 4 captaincy task was supposed to be about leadership. It turned into a masterclass in betrayal.

As contestants schemed to block rivals from the captain's chair, old friendships cracked in real time. Two housemates who entered as a pair are now barely speaking, and a secret deal made in the smoking area has become the talk of the house.

By the end of the task, the new captain was decided — but at the cost of at least three relationships. In the Bigg Boss house, the crown always comes with casualties.
