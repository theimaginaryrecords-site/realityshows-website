Isha Rikhi Evicted From Bigg Boss 20 in Week 3

September 28, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Isha Rikhi's Bigg Boss 20 journey ended on September 27, as she was evicted during Weekend Ka Vaar after receiving the fewest public votes. Only two contestants were nominated that week — Isha and Yung DSA — and the rapper was saved by the audience.

Salman Khan praised Isha for playing the game with dignity. In a post-exit note, she reflected: "Three weeks inside the Bigg Boss house... I had my emotional breakdowns, moments where I struggled to express myself."

Isha's stint was marked by her revelations about her separation from Badshah and her straightforward, no-drama gameplay. Her exit leaves the house without one of its calmest voices.

With three evictions done in four weeks, the race for the finale is tightening — and the fan vote grows more unpredictable each week.
