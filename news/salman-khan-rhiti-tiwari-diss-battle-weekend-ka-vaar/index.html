Salman Khan Destroys Rhiti Tiwari Over Diss Battle: "Worst Diss I've Seen"

September 28, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Salman Khan came down hard on Rhiti Tiwari during the September 26-27 Weekend Ka Vaar, over her conduct in a rap diss-battle task. In the task, Rhiti had directed derogatory, partially-muted and sexually suggestive remarks at Uditi Singh and Qazi Touqeer; Qazi lost his cool, throwing his jacket and banging objects.

"Honestly, this was one of the worst disses I've seen. Disrespect ki bhi ek limit hoti hai," Salman said, asking her how she would feel if the same words were aimed at her sister. He compared her to Dolly Bindra and warned about the dangers of reality-show notoriety.

The host did not spare the boys either — slamming Aasif Khan, ScoutOP and Yung DSA for laughing along and encouraging her: "Aap log chadha rahe the aur ye chadh rahi thi." To Aasif: "Agar aapki behen ke upar ye diss hota toh karne dete aap?"

In contrast, Salman praised Qazi and Uditi for their composure. Celebrity guests that weekend included Ajay Devgn, Jaideep Ahlawat and Fatima Shaikh.
