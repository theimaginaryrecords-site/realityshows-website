BB20: TV Actor Manish Goel Enters as Season’s First Wildcard on Weekend Ka Vaar

October 11, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Bigg Boss 20 has its first wildcard. Television actor Manish Goel entered the house during Saturday’s Weekend Ka Vaar, joining Salman Khan on stage before walking through the doors.

Salman and Manish played a prank on the housemates, making them believe the actor had come to promote an upcoming project — then revealed he was joining the game as a contestant. Sharing his first impressions, Manish called Mahhi Vij “calculative”; Mahhi appeared delighted to see him, as the two have known each other for years.

Manish is known for TV roles in Anupamaa, Bhabhi, Kahaani Ghar Ghar Kii and Kasautii Zindagii Kay, and competed with wife Poonam Narula on the first season of Nach Baliye, finishing as runners-up.

He may not be the only new face: the promo teased another entry, with creator Nakul Dhull’s name doing the rounds — but the makers haven’t confirmed it, so treat that as buzz until the show names him.
