Khatron Ke Khiladi 15 Grand Finale: Date, Time, Finalists — Winner Crowned Oct 3-4

October 2, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

It all comes down to this weekend. The grand finale of Khatron Ke Khiladi 15 airs on October 3 and 4 at 9 PM on Colors TV and JioHotstar, with Rohit Shetty crowning the winner of the Cape Town season.

The season began August 1 after a week-long delay caused by the TRP blackout, and 13 celebrities have battled heights, water, creepy-crawlies and their own nerves across two months of stunt television.

Reports on the exact finalist lineup conflict — some name Farrhana Bhatt among the top contenders, others list Rithvik Dhanjani or Orry — so treat any "final five" list as unconfirmed until the telecast. What is certain: the finale episodes will settle every rumour.
