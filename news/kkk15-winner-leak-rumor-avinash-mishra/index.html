KKK15 Winner "Leaked"? Avinash Mishra Rumoured Victor — But Nothing Is Official Yet

By Aabid Khan · October 2, 2026

CONFIRMED (October 4): The rumour was right — Avinash Mishra officially won Khatron Ke Khiladi 15, defeating Rithvik Dhanjani and Orry in the grand finale.

With the Khatron Ke Khiladi 15 finale days away, the rumour mill is in overdrive: multiple online reports claim Avinash Mishra has won the season, taking home the trophy, ₹20 lakh and a Maruti Suzuki Swift.

But here is why we are not calling it: the runner-up reports directly contradict each other — one widely-shared report names Farrhana Bhatt as runner-up, another insists the top three are Avinash, Orry and Rithvik Dhanjani. When asked, host Rohit Shetty refused to confirm anything.

Winner "leaks" for stunt shows have a patchy track record, and edited finale episodes can differ from shoot-day chatter. Until Colors airs the finale on October 4, every claim is unverified.

Verdict: rumour, not fact. Our fan poll remains open — vote for who YOU think deserves the trophy.
