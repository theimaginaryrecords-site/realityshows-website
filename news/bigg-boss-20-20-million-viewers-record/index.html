Bigg Boss 20 Creates History: Crosses 20 Million Weekly Viewers

September 30, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Bigg Boss 20 is officially a phenomenon. The show has crossed 20 million weekly streaming viewers, making it the first non-cricket property in 2026 to hit the milestone, according to viewership data.

The season premiered on September 6 with 6.4 million viewers on launch day and has grown every single week since — 16.8 million in its second week and now past the 20 million mark.

The "Fans Ka Faisla" pre-premiere twist, the diverse contestant mix — from boxing legend Mary Kom to gaming star ScoutOP — and high-voltage weekend episodes are being credited for the record run.

With 11 weeks still to go, trade watchers expect the finale to shatter every previous record for the franchise.
