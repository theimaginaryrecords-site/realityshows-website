Weekend Ka Vaar: Salman Khan Schools Contestants Over Groupism

September 27, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

Salman Khan did not hold back this weekend. The host took the housemates to task over the growing groupism that has split the Bigg Boss 20 house into camps.

In a no-holds-barred session, Salman questioned contestants who he said were "playing safe in groups instead of playing the game." Several housemates were left squirming as clips of their own contradictory statements were played back.

The episode ended with a stern warning: the audience votes for individuals, not groups. With eviction night approaching, the message could not have been clearer.
