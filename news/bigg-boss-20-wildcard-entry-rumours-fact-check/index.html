Bigg Boss 20 Wildcard: Reem Shaikh Rumours, Karan Wahi Denies, Neetu Bisht Prank Exposed

October 2, 2026 · RealityShows.in desk

With three evictions done, speculation is rife about wildcard entries shaking up the Bigg Boss 20 house. Here is what we actually know — and what is just noise.

TV actress Reem Shaikh's name is doing the rounds on social media as a possible wildcard entrant, but there is no official word and no date attached — treat this as unconfirmed speculation. Separately, pre-season reports suggested "Fans Ka Faisla" loser Rhiya Ahir could return as a wildcard; again, unconfirmed.

Two names can be ruled out. Karan Wahi — whose ex Uditi Singh is in the house — clarified that his viral "Wild Card" UNO post was just about the card game: "I am not coming in Bigg Boss... at least not this year." Content creator Neetu Bisht's "wildcard entry" announcement turned out to be a prank video.

Bottom line: as of October 2, there is no confirmed wildcard entry. We will update this story the moment the makers announce anything official.
